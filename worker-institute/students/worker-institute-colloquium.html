Worker Institute Colloquium

The Worker Institute Students Worker Institute Colloquium

The Worker Institute occasionally organizes two or three colloquia to highlight our work on the Cornell campus. Typically three-hour Friday morning events held on campus, colloquia feature a rich array of speakers and panelists and bring faculty and students together to discuss issues of common interest.
