Labor Roundtable

The Worker Institute Students Labor Roundtable

About the Event

The ILR Labor Roundtable is an annual event that brings a wide range of representatives in labor leadership, unions, and social justice organizations to campus to engage in dynamic, in-depth conversations with students. Through these discussions, students gain exposure to current trends in the labor movement and the fundamental role that it plays in bringing about social change.

The 2020 Labor Roundtable will be held in two separate sessions via Zoom. Each session will feature a different group of labor leaders, and students are welcome to attend one or both session. Please register for the session(s) you wish to attend using the links below (if you plan to attend both, you must complete both registration forms).

The event is open to students from all colleges and degree programs, as well as students from TC3 and Ithaca College. For questions about this year's program, contact ilrcareers@cornell.edu.
