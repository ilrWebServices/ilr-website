Visiting Scholar

Buffalo Co-Lab Initiatives Visiting Scholar

In 2018, the ILR Buffalo Co-Lab began its Visiting Scholar program to bring distinguished community leader from Buffalo into the classroom for a yearlong appointment directly interacting with students and faculty on the Ithaca campus on an ongoing basis. They provide career modeling for students interested in social justice activism and community leadership and facilitate new research and publications.
