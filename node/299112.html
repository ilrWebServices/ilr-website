Laura Holland

ILR '22

Partnership for the Public Good

High Road Fellowships - Buffalo, NY

“I decided to take the High Road this
summer because I wanted to work with
marginalized communities and help find
solutions to their issues. I have never
done research, or this kind of work,
before and I wanted to challenge
myself because that’s how you grow.
This fellowship helped me become a
better citizen, person, and professional
who can take these skills and help even
more communities.”

WORK HIGHLIGHTS

• Created a tool kit to advance implementation of recently passed progressive bail reform in NYS.

• Conducted interviews with the Erie County District Attorney, local defense attorneys and local activists to inform the tool kit.

• Did court observations in Buffalo City Court at bail hearings.

• Attended meetings of the Police Oversight Committee for the Buffalo Common Council.

• Represented PPG at a working group on Law Enforcement Assisted Diversion (LEAD).

• Completed workshops on the 2020 Census and working with interpreters. 

BUFFALO HIGHLIGHT

Picketing outside of Spot Coffee, fireworks on the 4th of July, T.I. concert at Canalside, Food is Art event, Juneteenth, and eating lots of wings and Paula’s donuts!

INTERESTS

Law, government, social justice, film, music.

CONVENTIONAL AND UNCONVENTIONAL SKILLS

Writing, public speaking, and film making. Volleyball, random knowledge from The Bachelor, and the ability to binge watch any show way too quickly.

BLOG POSTS

Lina Cheng

BSILR '14

I went on an incredible 10-day mid-semester break trip that took me up along the eastern coast of Australia. The trip is called One Fish Two Fish and I booked it through World Backpackers Travel Agency. If you do one thing in Australia this should definitely be it. You get to visit all the major Aussie tourist-y locations with other study abroad kids (there were 80 on my trip); I snorkeled/scuba dove the Great Barrier Reef, bungee jumped, saw kangaroos and koalas and other Aussie critters, went whitewater rafting, and explored some of the most beautiful beaches in the world. I can say without hesitation that it was among the best 10 days of my life.
