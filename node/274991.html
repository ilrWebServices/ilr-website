High Road Fellows

Buffalo Co-Lab Initiatives High Road Fellowships High Road Fellows

Since 2009, the High Road Fellowship Program has placed 209 Cornell students in the co-laboratory of Buffalo, N.Y. to learn, explore, and help revitalize the historic Rust Belt city. Take a look at the fellows below to learn more about previous High Road years and placements.
