Home Programs & Admissions Graduate Degree Programs Master of Industrial and Labor Relations (MILR) Visit Campus

 

January 6, 2021 at 1:00 pm (EST)

Join us for last minute application questions and tips!

  •  

Christie Avgar, Director of Graduate Enrollment and Student Services, and Darrie O'Connell, Recruiting and Outreach Coordinator, will be holding an interactive information session discussing admissions requirements and offering tips on completing your application. They will also be discussing the decision process and letting you know when you can expect to receive your admission decision.

Submit your questions in advance and ask questions during the event.

Christie and Darrie look forward to talking with you!

Register in advance for this event:
https://cornell.zoom.us/meeting/register/tJ0od-CsrzMuHNWWnl8VVQGPTmkla3UOSdMF

After registering, you will receive a confirmation email containing information about joining the event.

 

Important Notice: In response to the COVID-19 outbreak, Cornell University has canceled exisiting and prohibited campus tours and other in-person campus until further notice. This includes visits and events related to admissions and recruitment at ILR. To learn more about our programs as you navigate the admissions process, you can connect with our staff through virtual admissions events. We will continue to explore additional virtual event opportunities and will post updates to our MILR page. Thank you for your understanding and patience.


A helpful map of the ILR School is available at this link. For additional visitor information see the Cornell visitor website and Cornell University Maps. We also encourage you to Experience Cornell through video tours.

Applicants considering the MILR/MBA dual degree program should inquire about virtual admissions events with the SC Johnson College of Business. Please visit the Johnson School's website for more information.
